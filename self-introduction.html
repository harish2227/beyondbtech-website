👋 MOST COMMON FRESHER QUESTION

Tell Me
About Yourself

Don't worry about speaking perfect English. First understand what the interviewer actually wants to know.

🎯 First, What Is the Question?

"Tell me about yourself."

Ee question almost every fresher interview lo adugutharu. Kani ikkada interviewer ki mee complete life story kavali ani kaadu.

🧠 Mundu Question Meaning Ardham Cheskundam

Simple ga cheppalante, interviewer me gurinchi konni important things telusukovalani anukuntaru.

01 · WHO ARE YOU?

Mee name and basic background.

02 · EDUCATION

Meeru em chadivaru?

03 · SKILLS

Meeru nerchukunna skills enti?

04 · STRENGTHS

Mee positive qualities enti?

05 · GOAL

Meeru next em cheyyalani anukuntunnaru?

Anthe bro. Simple ga ee points cover chesthe manchi introduction prepare cheyyachu.

🔍 Interviewer Em Telusukovalani Anukuntaru?

Introduction lo interviewer mainly mee background, communication, skills and career interest gurinchi basic idea pondalani anukuntaru.

Kabatti introduction ni short and relevant ga unchadam better.

📌 Easy Structure to Follow

STEP 1

Greeting + Name

STEP 2

Education

STEP 3

Skills or Interest

STEP 4

Strength

STEP 5

Career Goal

Ee structure ni exact ga follow cheyyalsina rule emi ledu. Mee situation and experience batti change cheskovachu.

🗣️ You Can Say Like This

Ikkada simple English example undi. Idi only oka example. Same words memorize cheyyalsina avasaram ledu.

Good morning sir/madam. My name is ________.

I recently completed my bachelor's degree in ________.

I am interested in ________ and I am currently learning ________.

My strengths are that I am a hardworking person and I am willing to learn new things.

I am looking for an opportunity where I can improve my skills, learn from the team and contribute to the company.

Thank you.

✨ Example for a Fresher

Good morning sir/madam.

My name is Harish. I recently completed my bachelor's degree in Computer Science.

I am interested in Data Analytics and I am currently learning SQL and Power BI.

My strengths are that I am a hardworking person and I can adapt to new technologies.

I am looking for an opportunity where I can learn, improve my skills and contribute to the company.

Thank you.

💡 Idi example matrame bro. Mee education, skills and interests base cheskoni mee own introduction prepare cheskondi.

💡 Most Important Thing

Don't memorize every word.

Exact same answer gurthu pettukunte, interview lo oka word marchipoyina confuse ayye chance untundi.

Instead:

🧠 Main points ardham cheskondi
🗣️ Simple English lo practice cheyyandi
💪 Own words lo natural ga cheppandi

⚠️ Common Mistakes Beginners Make

❌ Complete life story cheppadam
Introduction short and relevant ga unchandi.
❌ Difficult English use cheyyadam
Simple English lo clearly cheppadam better.
❌ Answer ni robot laga memorize cheyyadam
Meaning ardham cheskoni natural ga cheppandi.
❌ Skills gurinchi false information cheppadam
Meeku telisina skills matrame mention cheyyandi.

🎤 Now Your Turn

Ippudu mee own introduction prepare cheyyandi using this structure:

Name → Education → Skills → Strength → Career Goal

First Telugu lo points think cheyyandi. Tarvata simple English lo slow ga practice cheyyandi.
