Hospital Management

10 SQL Practice Questions

01

Hospital management wants to know the total revenue generated by each doctor based on all bills tied to their appointments. Display only doctors whose total revenue is more than ₹20,000.

02

Management wants to rank doctors within each department based on the number of completed appointments. Identify the top-performing doctors in each department.

03

Display month-wise appointment volume and revenue for the year 2025. Include only months having more than 30 appointments.

04

Calculate the appointment cancellation percentage for each department. Display only departments with a cancellation rate above 15%.

05

Identify patients who have visited the hospital more than twice. Display their total number of appointments and total amount spent.

06

Calculate the cumulative monthly revenue over time to show the revenue growth trend.

07

Display the total paid, pending, and unpaid bill amounts for each department. Identify departments where the total unpaid amount exceeds ₹10,000.

08

Identify the top 3 most expensive treatments performed within each department.

09

Find all appointments for which no corresponding bill has been recorded.

10

Create a stored procedure named GETDOCTORREVEUE that accepts a start date and end date and returns doctor-wise revenue, including doctor name, department name, and total revenue for appointments within the specified date range.
