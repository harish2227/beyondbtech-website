Hospital Management

Business Scenario Questions

01

Revenue Analysis

BUSINESS SCENARIO

Hospital management wants to know the total revenue generated by each doctor, based on all bills tied to their appointments, but only for doctors who have generated more than ₹20,000 in total revenue.

Question: Calculate the total revenue generated by each doctor based on all bills tied to their appointments. Display only doctors whose total revenue is more than ₹20,000.
02

Doctor Performance Ranking

BUSINESS SCENARIO

Management wants to rank doctors within each department by number of completed appointments, to identify the top performer per department.

Question: Rank doctors within each department based on the number of completed appointments. Identify the top-performing doctors in each department.
03

Monthly Appointment & Revenue Trends

BUSINESS SCENARIO

The finance team wants to review month-wise appointment volume and revenue trends, but only for the year 2025.

Question: Display month-wise appointment volume and revenue for the year 2025. Include only months having more than 30 appointments.
04

Appointment Cancellation Analysis

BUSINESS SCENARIO

Operations wants to identify departments with unusually high appointment cancellation rates so they can investigate scheduling issues.

Question: Calculate the appointment cancellation percentage for each department. Display only departments with a cancellation rate above 15%.
05

Loyal Patient Analysis

BUSINESS SCENARIO

The hospital wants to identify loyal patients who have visited more than twice, to consider them for a follow-up or loyalty program.

Question: Identify patients who have visited the hospital more than twice. Display their total number of appointments and total amount spent.
06

Cumulative Revenue Trend

BUSINESS SCENARIO

Finance wants to track a running (cumulative) total of monthly revenue over time to visualize growth trends directly from a query.

Question: Calculate the cumulative monthly revenue over time to show the revenue growth trend.
07

Bill Status Analysis

BUSINESS SCENARIO

The billing team wants a breakdown of paid, pending, and unpaid bills by department, to flag departments with a high unpaid amount.

Question: Display the total paid, pending, and unpaid bill amounts for each department. Identify departments where the total unpaid amount exceeds ₹10,000.
08

Treatment Cost Ranking

BUSINESS SCENARIO

The hospital wants to identify the top 3 most expensive treatments performed within each department, for cost review and supplier negotiation.

Question: Identify the top 3 most expensive treatments performed within each department.
09

Missing Billing Records

BUSINESS SCENARIO

The hospital wants to find appointments that have no corresponding bill recorded, indicating a billing gap that needs correction.

Question: Find all appointments for which no corresponding bill has been recorded.
10

Doctor Revenue Stored Procedure

BUSINESS SCENARIO

Management wants a reusable T-SQL procedure that returns doctor-wise revenue for any given date range, without rewriting the query every time.

Question: Create a stored procedure named GETDOCTORREVEUE that accepts a start date and end date and returns doctor-wise revenue, including the doctor name, department name, and total revenue for appointments within the specified date range.
