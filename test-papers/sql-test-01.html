BUSINESS SCENARIO

Hospital management wants to know the total revenue generated by each doctor, based on all bills tied to their appointments, but only for doctors who have generated more than ₹20,000 in total revenue.

Question: Calculate the total revenue generated by each doctor based on all bills tied to their appointments. Display only doctors whose total revenue is more than ₹20,000.

BUSINESS SCENARIO

Management wants to rank doctors within each department by number of completed appointments, to identify the top performer per department.

Question: Rank doctors within each department based on the number of completed appointments. Identify the top-performing doctors in each department.

BUSINESS SCENARIO

The finance team wants to review month-wise appointment volume and revenue trends, but only for the year 2025.

Question: Display month-wise appointment volume and revenue for the year 2025. Include only months having more than 30 appointments.

BUSINESS SCENARIO

BUSINESS SCENARIO

BUSINESS SCENARIO
