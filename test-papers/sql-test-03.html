5 PRACTICE QUESTIONS

01
BUSINESS SCENARIO

A product team wants to identify the most engaged users by measuring how many consecutive days in a row each user logged in, to power a "streak" feature or loyalty reward.

Question: Find the longest consecutive streak of daily logins for each user.
02
BUSINESS SCENARIO

Sales leadership wants a month-by-month leaderboard so each representative's rank reflects only that month's performance, not a cumulative total. This is used to calculate the "Salesperson of the Month" award.

Question: Rank salespeople by monthly sales, resetting the rank every month.
03
BUSINESS SCENARIO

HR wants to identify the longest-serving employee in each department for service awards, succession planning, and knowledge-retention analysis.

Question: Find employees who have the longest tenure within their department.
04
BUSINESS SCENARIO

Category managers want a recurring monthly report of the top 3 best-selling products by revenue to guide inventory replenishment and promotional planning for the following month.

Question: Find the top 3 products with the highest total sales amount each month.
05
BUSINESS SCENARIO

Customer support wants a quick way to retrieve a customer's three most recent orders when they call, without browsing their complete order history.

Question: Get the last 3 orders placed by each customer.
